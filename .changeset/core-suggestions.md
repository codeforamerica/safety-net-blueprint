A contract set can now include suggestions by listing the `suggestions` capability in `x-capabilities`: `/decision-support/suggestions` records something proposed to a person — a call to an operation the contracts declare, or something pointed out about a record in a category the set declares in a `suggestion-categories` registry. A person decides one with `accept`, `dismiss` (with a reason) or `expire`, for one that no longer applies. Accepting a call makes it as the person accepting, or makes the corrected call they send, which must name the same operation and is kept as `performed`. A call that fails leaves the suggestion `refused`, its `refused` event carrying the call and the error, to accept again. A suggestion expires at its `expiresAt`, a day after it was proposed unless it says otherwise, on a scheduler timer that deciding it cancels. The call's shape, `OperationCall`, is in `base/schemas/operations.yaml` for any contract to use. (#469)

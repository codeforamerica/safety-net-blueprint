Before, a transcription session could only be tried one call at a time, with lines typed in by hand. Now its start and resume take audio — the microphone, a recording, or typed lines spoken in the page — which the session's adapter, routed in the site's `routing:`, hears when it asks the browser for the microphone, and the adapter's calls and events show in the activity log.

facts interviewPrompts.changeVerificationPrompt
Identify and document all changes in household circumstances that occurred between the application date and the interview date, and determine what verification is required.
facts interviewPrompts.hasNonCitizenMembers
Identifies non-citizen household members who must be screened for qualified alien status and sponsor income deeming rules.
facts interviewPrompts.hasStudentMembers
Identifies household members enrolled at least half-time in higher education who are subject to student eligibility restrictions.
facts interviewPrompts.hasUnemployedWorkEligible
Identifies household members aged 16–59 who are not employed and have not yet been screened for general work requirement exemptions.
facts interviewPrompts.immigrationStatusPrompt
Determine qualified alien status and apply sponsor income and resource deeming rules for non-citizen household members.
facts interviewPrompts.incomeGapExists
Flags a potential inconsistency when reported income does not cover reported expenses. Required by FNS-1104 as a probe trigger — workers must probe for undisclosed income sources or assistance.
facts interviewPrompts.incomeInconsistencyPrompt
Probe for undisclosed income sources or assistance when monthly income is less than monthly expenses.
facts interviewPrompts.studentEligibilityPrompt
Screen higher-education students for SNAP eligibility exceptions — most students enrolled at least half-time are ineligible unless they meet one of the statutory exceptions.
facts interviewPrompts.workRequirementPrompt
Screen non-employed household members aged 16–59 for general work requirement exemptions before determining work registration obligations.

Decision Support State Machine

Domain: decision-support · API: decision-support-suggestions-openapi.yaml

Suggestion

States

StateDescriptionSLA clock
proposed Waiting for a person to decide. stopped
accepted A person said yes, and for an operation suggestion, the call was made. Terminal. stopped
refused A person said yes, and the call could not be made. It can be accepted again until it expires. stopped
dismissed A person said no, with a reason. Terminal. stopped
expired It stopped applying before anyone decided. Terminal. stopped

counted-hours 7 usages
Hours count only when the person says they do that activity: one fact each for working, volunteering and training hours, 0 when the answer is no. Each is its own fact because the engine settles a condition only at the top of an expression, not inside a sum. So someone who doesn't work, volunteer or train is never asked for hours. The tool adds the hours whatever the answer, but clears them on save when the answer is no.

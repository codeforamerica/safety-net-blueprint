# A recertification interview

Fictional. Written in the same format as the transcription tools' demo
scripts, so any of theirs can be played here instead: each line starts
`**Worker.**` or `**Applicant.**`.

## Script

**Worker.** Thanks for calling back. This is the county benefits office, and I'm going to ask a few questions to update your case.
**Applicant.** Sure, that's fine.
**Worker.** Can you tell me who lives with you right now?
**Applicant.** It's me, my two kids, and my mom moved in last month.
**Worker.** Thank you. Is anyone in the household working?
**Applicant.** I am. I started at the grocery store in March, about thirty hours a week.
**Worker.** And how often are you paid?
**Applicant.** Every two weeks.
**Worker.** Does your mom have any income, like Social Security?
**Applicant.** Yes, she gets Social Security, I think around eleven hundred a month.
**Worker.** That's everything I need for now. I'll send you a notice once the review is done.
**Applicant.** Okay, thank you.
